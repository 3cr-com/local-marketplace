# NTN-BZR-7 — Delivery Partner Workspace

## Purpose

Add a dedicated operational role for delivery partners to accept assigned work, navigate pickup/drop-off details, update delivery status, and view their delivery history.

## Planned scope

- Delivery-partner role, invitation/approval process, active/inactive access, and audit trail.
- Assignment model linking a delivery partner to an order with pickup/drop-off snapshots.
- Mobile-first queue: assigned, pickup, in transit, completed, and failed delivery states.
- OTP/proof-of-delivery and customer-safe contact handling.
- Admin controls for assignment, capacity, service area, partner availability, earnings, and dispute handling.

## Not started

This needs an operational policy for assignment, payments, proof of delivery, and partner safety before schema work begins.
