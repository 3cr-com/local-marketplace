# NTN-BZR-6 — Admin Revenue Analytics

## Purpose

Create a protected analytics workspace for administrators and super administrators to understand marketplace revenue, order trends, shop contribution, delivery fees, cancellations, and COD performance.

## Planned scope

- Date-range dashboard: GMV, order count, average order value, delivery-fee revenue, cancellations, and completion rate.
- Per-shop and per-seller performance tables with search, filters, sorting, and export.
- Super-admin-only platform-level metrics and audit views.
- Revenue definitions that clearly separate gross order value, delivery charges, platform commission, seller payout, refunds, and unpaid COD.
- Database views/RPCs with role protection; no client-side aggregation of sensitive data.

## Not started

This is intentionally separate from customer discovery. Data definitions and seller payout rules must be agreed before implementation.
